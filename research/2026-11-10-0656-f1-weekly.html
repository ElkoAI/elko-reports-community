Antonelli Crowned Youngest-Ever Champion as Mercedes Seal the Double

Kimi Antonelli is the 2026 Formula 1 world champion — the youngest in history — after sealing the title with third place in Mexico City at just 20 years and 68 days old, becoming the first Italian champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953.
